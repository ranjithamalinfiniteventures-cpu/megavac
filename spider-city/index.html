SPIDER CITY

Swing · Drive · Fly
Building the city…

MOVE

Drag on the left side to walk, push the stick all the way to sprint (or tap RUN).
Drag on the right side to look around.

WEB

Hold SWING to swing from buildings, let go to fling. JUMP jumps.
WEB zips you to the centre of the screen. Run into walls to climb.

RIDES

PHONE calls a car, plane or tank, or walk up to any car and tap DRIVE.
Cars: the stick steers, GAS, BRAKE and NITRO drive.

FIGHT

GUN switches to the blaster, FIRE shoots, BOMB throws a grenade.
Crimes bring the police (stars at the top): lose them or take them down.

MISSIONS

Walk into a glowing beam (coloured dots on the map) to start a mission for cash.
SLOW turns on slow motion.
