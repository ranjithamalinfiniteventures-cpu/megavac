  • Move the mouse, or drag on touch, to steer the nozzle.
  • Suck up debris and clean the floor — a room finishes when both are done.
  • Hold the nozzle over heavy objects to break them down. Crush upgrades make them quicker to handle.
  • Chain pickups without pausing to build a combo multiplier for more coins.
  • Battery Challenge drains as you play. Clear rooms and buy Battery or Efficiency to last longer.
  • Mess of the Day is a date-seeded personal challenge — three tries, best time kept.
  • Buy new vacuums in the Shop. Improve your vacuum in the Garage. Upgrades stay with you when you change machines.
  • Finish five jobs in each location to unlock the next. Use the destination cards to replay unlocked locations.
  • Music: Carefree by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com, CC-BY 4.0). Toggle music anytime with B or the 🎵 button; toggle sound with M or the 🔊 button.
  • Space or the ⚡ button fires a short Turbo boost to reach, suction, and nozzle width. It has its own cooldown. P or Esc pauses.
